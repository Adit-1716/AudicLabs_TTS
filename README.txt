---------------
  1. Read the brief end to end, including the Evaluation Criteria and
     Additional Requirements.
     Every requirement is mandatory.
  2. Read the AudicLabs docs at https://docs.audiclabs.com (APIs,
     Authentication, Errors, Rate limits).
  3. Your personal API key is sent separately by the hiring team. Put it

     we look at your commit history.

     in your README, and carry on. You can also reply to the assessment
     email with questions.
